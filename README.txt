PRINCE PHARMA WEBSITE - SETUP NOTES
1. Replace  https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com  with your real website address in:
   index.html, robots.txt, sitemap.xml   (Find & Replace, all occurrences)
2. WhatsApp number: edit CONFIG at the top of script.js
3. Hindi / English: all Hindi text and the WhatsApp welcome messages are in i18n.js.
   To change a Hindi sentence, edit the text on the right side of that line.
   Share a Hindi link directly:  https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/?lang=hi
4. Verify the address in index.html (search "Bishambharpur") matches your Google Business Profile.
5. Upload the whole folder (index.html, style.css, script.js, i18n.js, images/, favicon.ico, robots.txt, sitemap.xml).
6. In Google Search Console: submit sitemap.xml, then "Request indexing" for the home page.
